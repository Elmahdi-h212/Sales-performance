# Business Insights

The dashboard was created to monitor retail sales performance across time, locations, categories and customers.

## Main observations
- 2024 is the highest-sales year in the dashboard analysis.
- Marrakech-Safi is the leading region by sales in the analysis.
- Technologie represents the largest sales category.
- Sales increase through 2024 and then decline in 2025.
- The dashboard allows users to filter the analysis by year, city and category.

## Dashboard KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Quantity
- Total Customers
